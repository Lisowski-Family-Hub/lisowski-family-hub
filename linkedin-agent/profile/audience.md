# Audience

> **SEEDED DEFAULT** — refine via `questionnaire.md`. The drafter writes for
> exactly the reader described here.

## Primary reader

Managers and rising leaders in automotive retail and adjacent customer-facing
industries — sales managers, service leads, GMs, and the salespeople who want
those jobs. They're practical, time-poor, and allergic to corporate fluff.

## What they come to LinkedIn for

- Playbook material: things they can try with their team this week.
- Proof they're not alone in the hard parts — turnover, tough months, difficult
  conversations.
- Signals about where the industry is going (EVs, digital retail, changing
  customer expectations) translated into "what do I do Monday."

## Secondary readers

- Peers and senior leaders (credibility audience — they won't comment much, but
  they're watching for judgment and consistency).
- Potential hires (culture audience — posts double as a recruiting signal).

## Writing implications

- Assume floor-level fluency: don't explain what a walkaround or a CSI score
  is; do explain anything corporate or technical.
- Respect their time: one idea per post, no throat-clearing.
- The comment-section question should be answerable from their own experience
  in under a minute.
