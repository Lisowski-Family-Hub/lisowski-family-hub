# Content pillars

Five pillars, one per weekday. The Sunday drafter follows this mapping exactly;
the validator enforces the pillar ids.

| Weekday | Pillar id | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | `personal-lesson` | A leadership lesson learned the hard way — a real moment, what it cost, what changed |
| Tuesday | `industry-pov` | A sharp point of view on where the industry is heading and what leaders should do about it |
| Wednesday | `team-culture` | A story about building teams and culture — hiring, coaching, trust, accountability |
| Thursday | `contrarian-take` | Respectfully disagree with a piece of common wisdom, and argue the alternative |
| Friday | `celebrating-others` | Spotlight someone else's work — a teammate, a peer, a mentor — and what others can learn from them |

## Guidance per pillar

- **personal-lesson** — First person, specific scene, real stakes. The lesson
  should be earned by the story, not bolted on. Vulnerability beats polish.
- **industry-pov** — One claim, clearly stated in the first line, backed by
  what you're seeing on the ground. No trend-listicles.
- **team-culture** — Center a person or a moment, not a framework. The reader
  should finish thinking "I could try that with my team tomorrow."
- **contrarian-take** — Steelman the common view before dismantling it. Punch
  at ideas, never at people or named companies.
- **celebrating-others** — Name them (when appropriate), be concrete about what
  they did, and extract the transferable lesson. Zero self-congratulation.

## Rotation rules

- One post per weekday, pillar fixed by the table above — consistency over novelty.
- Within a pillar, never reuse a topic or hook that appears in the last 10
  files of `published/`.
- If `performance/log.md` shows a pillar consistently underperforming, sharpen
  the angle within that pillar rather than swapping pillars — the cadence is
  the brand.
